Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Version 1.0 (beta)

Acceptable use

Use ToknShield only for lawful, authorised purposes. Do not use it to:

  • commit fraud or gain unauthorised access to systems or data
  • violate anyone's privacy
  • create or spread malware
  • harass others
  • evade legal obligations

Also, don't:

  • interfere with ToknShield's security
  • distribute altered versions presented as the official software
  • test systems you don't own without permission

Responsible use of AI

ToknShield is a privacy aid, not permission to submit confidential material to an AI provider. Before you use it:

  • get any authorisations you need
  • follow your workplace and professional duties
  • review the terms of the AI service you send content to

Vulnerability disclosure

Security researchers are welcome to report suspected vulnerabilities to tom@capinora.com. Please include:

  • the affected versions
  • reproducible steps
  • the impact

Use synthetic test data only.

Please don't:

  • access other people's data
  • take data you aren't entitled to
  • disrupt services
  • publish exploitable details before we've had a chance to fix them

We'll acknowledge your report within 5 business days. We ask for up to 90 days to release a fix before details are published, and we'll agree a disclosure date with you.

If you follow this policy in good faith, we won't pursue action against you for your research.

Enforcement

We may investigate credible reports of misuse and take proportionate action, consistent with the law and our Terms of Use. To appeal a restriction, email tom@capinora.com.
